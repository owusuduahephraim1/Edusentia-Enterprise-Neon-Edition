Account recovery

System Administrators who have forgotten a password can use Forgot password? on the school sign-in screen. If an authenticator is lost, use an available one-time MFA recovery code or request a protected authenticator reset through a verified support channel.

School onboarding and provisioning

New school registrations, licence activation and tenant provisioning are processed through the protected Edusentia control plane. Provide only the school and primary-contact details requested by the official workflow.

Backup, restore and operational support

Backup and restore requests involving protected school data should be initiated through authorised administrative controls or a verified support channel. Recovery operations are validated before protected data or access is changed.

Security reminder

Never send passwords, authenticator secrets, one-time recovery codes, database credentials or private activation material through an unverified channel. Support may ask you to confirm school and contact identity, but should not require you to disclose a current password.
