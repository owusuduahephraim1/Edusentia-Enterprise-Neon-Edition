Terms

Edusentia Enterprise authorised-use terms.

Access is limited to authorised institutions and users. Use of the platform must respect assigned roles, security controls, licensing conditions and legitimate school purposes.

Authorised access and acceptable use

Users must use Edusentia only through accounts, roles and tenant environments they are authorised to access. Attempts to bypass authentication, licensing, tenant isolation, security controls or administrative restrictions are prohibited.

Credentials and multi-factor authentication

Users are responsible for protecting passwords, authenticator access and recovery codes associated with their accounts. Privileged administrators must follow the platform's MFA and recovery procedures and should report suspected credential compromise promptly.

Licensing, capacity and availability

Tenant access is governed by the active Edusentia licence period, authorised capacity and plan entitlements. The platform may restrict operations after licence expiry, suspension or revocation while preserving protected records according to the system's continuity and recovery controls.

School data and institutional responsibility

Participating schools are responsible for the legitimacy, accuracy and authorised use of information they submit to Edusentia. Users must handle academic and administrative information only for legitimate institutional purposes and in accordance with applicable school policy and law.

Security, backup and recovery

Edusentia provides security, backup and recovery mechanisms intended to support operational continuity, but authorised users remain responsible for using those controls correctly, protecting recovery material and following approved restoration procedures.

Service changes

Features, release requirements, supported integrations and operational controls may evolve as the platform is maintained. Material operational changes should be introduced through the Edusentia release and deployment process.

Operational summary

These product terms are an operational summary and may later be replaced or supplemented by formal commercial or legal terms issued by the software owner. Questions may be submitted through the verified channels on the Support page.
