Privacy

School information is handled as protected operational data.

Edusentia uses tenant isolation, restricted privileged operations, accountable administrative actions and protected backup and recovery controls to reduce exposure of school information.

Information processed by the platform

Depending on the modules used by a participating school, Edusentia may process student, staff, guardian, academic, attendance, assessment, finance, communication, identity, authentication, audit, document and operational records supplied or generated through the school environment.

Tenant isolation and access control

Tenant schools use isolated Neon PostgreSQL databases. Access is governed by authenticated identities, assigned roles, least-privilege service roles and additional multi-factor authentication requirements for privileged administrators. Administrative and security-sensitive operations are recorded where the platform provides audit evidence.

Storage, files and backups

Structured application data is hosted in Neon PostgreSQL. Protected files and managed package objects are stored through Cloudflare R2. Backup and recovery workflows are designed to preserve tenant boundaries and require authorised recovery paths.

Public verification and minimum disclosure

Where Edusentia provides public verification for authorised academic documents, the verification surface should expose only the information needed to validate the document or record rather than unrestricted access to the underlying tenant data.

School responsibilities

Individual schools remain responsible for the lawful collection, accuracy, authorised use and appropriate retention of the student, staff, parent or guardian information they place in their own tenant environment, including any notices or permissions required under applicable law or institutional policy.

Security and service telemetry

The platform may process technical logs, request identifiers, release-health information and security events needed to operate, diagnose and protect the service. Such operational information should be limited to what is necessary for reliability, accountability and security.
