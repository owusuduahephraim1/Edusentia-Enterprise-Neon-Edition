QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Which institutions can use Edusentia Enterprise?

The current Neon Edition supports configurations for Basic/JHS and Senior High School institutions. Feature availability may depend on the school's configured plan and rollout stage.

Can parents or third parties verify official documents?

Supported issued documents can be checked through the public verification portal. An issued QR code is a link to a live status check, not proof of validity on its own.

Can the platform be used on a phone?

Edusentia is designed for desktop and mobile browser use. Available modules depend on the authenticated user's role and the institution's configuration.

How do we arrange a demonstration?

Use the demonstration contact button to reach the platform administration team. Contact details and a dedicated domain email service are being finalized.
