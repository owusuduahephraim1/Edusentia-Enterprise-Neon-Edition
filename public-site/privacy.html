WEBSITE PRIVACY

A clear approach to this public website.

Information about the public-facing Edusentia Enterprise website. The authenticated school application has a separate data-handling context.

Last reviewed: 26 November 2026

Scope

This notice describes the public marketing website at edusentia.com. It does not replace a school-specific privacy notice, subscription agreement, or the authenticated application's privacy and data-processing documentation.

Information you choose to share

This website does not include a contact form or request account credentials, student grades or other school records. If you choose to follow the external WhatsApp link, the message you send is handled by WhatsApp and the Edusentia contact recipient under their respective practices.

Technical delivery

Hosting and security infrastructure may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request times, and browser characteristics when serving pages or protecting the service. The public website's own JavaScript does not include third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Links to other services

The buttons for the authenticated application and public document verification open separate Edusentia services. Access, session, and record-processing policies for those services depend on their operation and authorization rules.

Contact and updates

For website-related questions, use the public Edusentia platform contact channel. A dedicated custom-domain email address will be published after the receiving service has been configured and verified. This notice may be updated to reflect changes to the website and its operational arrangements.

This website notice is intentionally limited to facts about the public information site. Before wider commercial onboarding, obtain a legal and data-protection review covering controller/processor roles, lawful bases, international transfers, retention, data-subject rights and the authenticated school product.
